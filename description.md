# Glyphyx — store copy

## Short description (139 / 150 chars)

Drag glowing rune stones onto a 4×4 board, swipe to aim, and watch both sides
fire at once. Hold 8 tiles to win in ninety seconds.

## Description (486 / 500 chars)

Glyphyx is a 90-second tactical duel on a 4×4 stone board. Every turn you drag
one rune pebble onto a tile and swipe the direction it faces — and so does your
enemy, in secret. Then everything fires at once: swords strike, bows skip a tile,
arcane beams cut diagonals, shields absorb, crosses heal. Stack two matching
runes into a Level 2 powerhouse, shatter enemy stones into rubble, and claim
their tiles. Hold eight and you win. Fight three factions at once in the siege
campaign, chain victories for up to 3× gold, and unlock new runes and skins.

## How to play

1. **One rune per turn.** Your hand holds three pebbles. Drag one onto any free
   tile — or tap it, then tap the tile. Drop it onto one of your own runes of
   the same kind to stack it: each stack adds a level, up to **Level 8**, with
   more health and damage every time.
2. **Flick to aim.** While still holding, a short flick sets the facing —
   swords and bows aim up / down / left / right, the arcane orb aims along the
   diagonals; flick again to change your mind. Shields and crosses need no aim.
   Release to lock it in — and for one more second you can press anywhere and
   flick to re-aim the rune you just placed.
3. **Both sides plan in secret**, then the board reveals every move at once and
   glowing arrows show where each attack will go.
4. **Everything fires together**, in a fixed order: shields, then heals, then
   ranged (bows and orbs), then swords, then the tiles change hands.
   * **Sword** — hits the tile it faces (Lv 2 knocks the survivor back).
   * **Bow** — skips one tile and hits the next (Lv 2 fires at two tiles).
   * **Arcane Orb** — a diagonal beam through two tiles (Lv 2 explodes at the end).
   * **Shield** — a wall: absorbs 1 from every hit, stops beams and intercepts
     arrows that fly over it (Lv 2 shields its neighbours too).
   * **Radiant Cross** — heals its neighbours every turn (Lv 2 also sharpens their attack).
5. **Runes with no health left shatter**, and their tile goes neutral. A rune
   standing on a tile owns it. Two pebbles dropped on the same free tile
   **clash** — the tougher one survives.
6. **Hold eight tiles** at the end of a turn to win instantly. After ten turns
   the side holding more tiles wins; a tie goes to sudden death.
7. **The siege.** In 1v3 stages you start surrounded in the centre by Orc
   Berserkers, Goblin Archers and Undead Mages. Break out to eight tiles, or
   still hold the line when the last turn ends.
8. **Win streaks pay.** Every consecutive victory lights the flame around your
   board and multiplies your gold, up to 3×. Lose once and it resets.
9. **The forge works while you're away.** Coins accrue for up to eight hours
   offline — tap the forge to collect them.
10. **The shop.** Rune skins change the stone your runes are carved from;
    power runes make your first placement of that type land at Level 3 in your
    next match. Both cost gold, or one short video. Clear stages to unlock new
    runes and skins from their chests. Progress saves itself after every match.
11. **Rank your runes up, permanently.** Every rune has five ranks, and each one
    gives it another point of health for good — the same for every rune, so no
    rune runs away with the game. Pay in gold, or with one short video. And one
    rank, on one rune, is **free** at any moment: a different rune is picked
    every twenty minutes, so there is always one waiting when you come back.

## Controls

| Action | Touch | Mouse |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Place a rune | Drag a pebble from your hand onto a tile | Click-and-drag a pebble onto a tile |
| Aim it | Keep holding and flick in the direction it should face, then release | Keep the button down, move a little in the direction it should face, then release |
| Re-aim the last rune | Within one second of releasing, press anywhere and flick, or tap one of the arrows around the stone | Within one second, press the button anywhere and drag a little, or press an arrow key / WASD (Q E Z C for the orb's diagonals) |
| Place by tapping | Tap a pebble in your hand, then tap a tile (you get 2.5 s to turn it) | Click a pebble, then click a tile |
| Reroll your hand | Tap the reroll stone (twice per match) | Click the reroll stone |
| Campaign map | Tap the stage badge at the top | Click the stage badge |
| Rune skins | Tap the gem button (bottom right) | Click the gem button |
| Options / language / audio | Tap the gear (bottom left) | Click the gear |
| Mute | Tap the speaker (bottom left) | Click the speaker |
| Close a panel | Tap the ✕ or outside it | **Esc** |

The game starts straight into stage 1-1 — there is no menu, and the first six
stages are short lessons (one per rune) that cannot be lost.
